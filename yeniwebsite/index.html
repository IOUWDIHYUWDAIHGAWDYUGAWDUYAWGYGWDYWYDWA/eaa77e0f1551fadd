Features

One bot. Everything covered.

VYBot removes the burden of running five separate bots at once. Each module is set up with a single command, managed from a single place.

Moderation

Keep order with a single command.

Ban, kick, timeout, warnings and records — all with SQLite-backed persistent logs. Every action is automatically posted to the mod-log channel.

  • Permanent record: Warnings and punishments are never lost.
  • Bulk cleanup: Delete 1–100 messages at once.
  • Auto logging: Every step is recorded as verifiable evidence.

Security

Your server, shielded.

Anti-nuke, anti-link, anti-swear and anti-spam shields scan message flow in real time. Unauthorized channel or role deletion attempts are blocked instantly.

  • Anti-Nuke: Stops mass-deletion attempts automatically.
  • Verification panel: Filters bots and fake accounts with button-based registration.
  • Comprehensive mod-log: Message, member, channel and role events.
